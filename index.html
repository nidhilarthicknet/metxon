Corporate Origins & History

Metxon was described in the supplied blueprint as a fictional multinational conglomerate. The earlier wiki history records a founding date of November 15, 2024 and a major restructure on January 28, 2026. The main public-facing name is now Metxon Corporate.

The original blueprint also describes a “Golden Banking Era” and the emergence of the four core divisions below.
