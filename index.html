Corporate Origins & History

Founding & The 2026 Structural Restructure

Launched initially on November 15, 2024, as an unconventional banking asset boasting consumer-first incentive paradigms, Metxon executed a massive operational pivot on January 28, 2026. Retiring completely from retail banking operations, Metxon transformed into a clean parent holding matrix, splitting its core architecture across exactly four core subsidiaries.
