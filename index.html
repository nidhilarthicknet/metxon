MetCount™ - Financial Infrastructure & Regulatory Framework

Metcount is the core financial ledger and regulatory framework governing the Mexton ecosystem and Mettrade. It enforces absolute transparency for public-facing financial assets while preserving operational security for private R&D.

Core Principles of Metcount

Mandatory Consumer & Market Publicity

Any corporate action impacting public consumers, active stock markets, or commercialized products must be fully disclosed on Mettrade, including public stock buy/sell events, official corporate announcements, and finished hardware/software releases.

Off-Ledger Protection for Private R&D

Metcount strictly excludes non-public initiatives from public disclosure. Internal defense protocols, early-stage silicon developments (like the APX Processor), and pre-release prototypes remain confidential until official rollout.

No Hidden Financial Transfers

Arbitrary capital payouts cannot occur off-market if public stocks or registered asset accounts are involved. Monetizing code or assets through public channels immediately logs the entire financial history onto Mettrade.
